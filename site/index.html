A hundred points, seven categories, and rules that can overrule the total.

Every company here is put through the same process. Business quality, growth, earnings, valuation, competitive position, catalysts and risk each receive a score out of ten, and each carries a fixed weight. Valuation carries as much weight as quality, because an excellent business bought at the wrong price is still a poor investment.

The total does not decide on its own. A high-quality business trading with no margin of safety cannot be labelled a buy. A cheap business with weak economics cannot be called attractive. A broken thesis overrules the score entirely. Those rules are applied by the software, not by mood.
